The Inner Journey

Here, there's no test to pass, no boxes to tick to define yourself. Just a journey, through scenes from your everyday life.

Along the way, your profile takes shape, little by little — your unique way of functioning, what carries you forward, what weighs on you when stress rises.

Your type is your starting point: it doesn't change over the course of your life. But it can be lived in a thousand different ways. Someone who has matured deeply remains the same type — they simply express its best, freed from their automatic patterns.

For example: someone who was once very much a perfectionist, now more flexible and at peace, remains deeply of this type — they have simply moved beyond its rigidity, without losing any of their sense of what is right.

“The personality mistakes imitation for the real. The journey begins when we dare to look behind the image.”— A.H. Almaas

Simply answer with your first instinct, without worrying about the “right answer”. There isn't one. Let yourself be carried along.

A journey, not a verdict.
