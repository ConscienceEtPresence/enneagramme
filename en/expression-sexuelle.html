The sexual expression of the 9 types

Sexuality as a revealer of deep structure: fears, defenses, essential quality, and path of presence.

Sexuality is not only a physical experience. It is a revealer of the deep structure of each type: its fears, its defenses, its way of loving, its way of being present or absent, its relationship to the body, to desire, to vulnerability, and to the other.

Each type has an essential quality that naturally enriches intimacy, but this quality is often veiled by the mechanisms of the ego. The work consists of returning to more presence, receptivity, inner truth, and openness of heart.

Sexuality becomes enriched when the person is present, open, connected to body, heart, and mind. It becomes diminished when the type operates from its central fear, its defensive automatisms, or its need to control the experience. Exploring the sexual expression of each enneatype is therefore a way of traveling a path of intimacy — with oneself first, then with the other.

General synthesis

The book shows that sexuality is a very powerful revealer of the ego. Each type replays its central mechanism there.

Fundamentally, for the authors, conscious sexuality is not only a matter of desire or technique. It is a path of presence. It requires inhabiting one's body, recognizing one's fears, communicating with honesty, receiving as much as giving, and letting the other meet us right where the ego usually defends itself. This is why the chapter constantly links sexuality, intimacy, personal development, and the essential qualities of each type.
